Calendar Dot

A personal assistant that watches its owner's Google Calendar and keeps them posted on WhatsApp.

What it does

  • Watches the owner's primary Google Calendar for today and the next 29 days.
  • When an upcoming meeting changes (new invitation, new time, cancellation, new location or meeting link), decides whether it matters and notifies the owner on WhatsApp.
  • Answers the owner's questions about their calendar, on WhatsApp or in a local chat window on the owner's computer.

Actions only with the owner's approval

Calendar Dot can propose an action: answer an invitation, cancel or move a meeting the owner organizes, create a meeting, or send a plain-text email from the owner's Gmail account. Nothing is changed or sent until the owner approves that exact action on WhatsApp with a one-time code. Recipients are limited to an allowlist the owner configures.

Who it is for

Calendar Dot is a personal, non-commercial project used only by its developer, Can Zagnos. It runs on the developer's own computer. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

Google data

Calendar Dot reads calendar events and the signed-in account's identity, and uses Gmail only to send emails the owner approved. It never reads the mailbox. Details are in the privacy policy.
